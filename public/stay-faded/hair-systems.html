Frequently Asked Questions

Nikkole is a certified hair system specialist and hair loss specialist who helps men dealing with thinning hair, receding hairlines, and hair loss find a natural-looking, non-surgical solution.

Is a hair system right for me?

Hair systems can be a strong option for men dealing with balding or thinning hair. They can also help you explore different hairlines, shapes, and styles before choosing a more permanent path. A consultation with Nikkole is the best way to understand what fits your goals.

Do you offer hair system installation?

Yes. We can help with the consultation, system selection, installation, cut, blend, and styling so you leave with a natural-looking result that fits your lifestyle.

Are you hair loss specialists?

We are certified hair system specialists who help men dealing with thinning hair, receding hairlines, and hair loss explore a confident, non-surgical solution.

Can I swim, shower, and exercise with a hair system?

Yes. With the right fit and care routine, you can shower, swim, exercise, sweat, and live normally in a hair system. Stay Faded will show you how to care for it so it stays secure, natural-looking, and comfortable.

Will people see my hair system?

The goal is an undetectable result, even up close. Your system is selected, customized, cut, and blended to match your features, existing hair, and lifestyle.

How long does a hair system last?

Many hair systems last about 3–6 months. The exact lifespan depends on the system materials, maintenance routine, skin, and lifestyle. Nikkole will explain what to expect for your specific system.

How is a hair system applied?

The scalp is prepared first, then the system is carefully fitted and secured with a safe, durable adhesive selected for a comfortable and natural-looking result.

Is it human hair?

Yes. Hair systems are made with real human hair. When needed, synthetic fibers may be blended in to create a natural-looking gray percentage or a specific finished look.
