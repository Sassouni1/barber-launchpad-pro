MEET Nikkole Abila

I'm #koleworldkuts, founder of Stay Faded Barbershop, and I've spent over 16 years behind the chair helping people look their best and feel confident.

But Stay Faded is more than a barbershop.

It's built on legacy, resilience, and love.

After losing my cousin Salvador, I realized life is too short to keep waiting for permission to live life on my own terms. Salvador lived authentically, loved deeply, carried positivity wherever he went, and always believed in showing up as yourself. A fresh haircut wasn't just a haircut to him—it was confidence.

If I'm being honest, owning a barbershop was never the dream. But losing Salvador changed my perspective. It made me realize that life is too short to spend it building someone else's vision when you're capable of building something of your own.

Stay Faded is my way of honoring his legacy.

At the same time, it's a reflection of my own journey. The years spent raising my children, building a career behind the chair, navigating grief and loss, and finding the courage to finally bet on myself.

This shop isn't about replacing the people we've lost. It's about carrying them with us. Every haircut, every conversation, every laugh, and every client who sits in my chair becomes part of that story.

When you walk into Stay Faded, you'll see a barbershop. But for me, you'll be walking into a place built from perseverance, family, purpose, and love.

It's a tribute to Salvador, a new beginning for me, and proof that even after loss, something beautiful can still grow.

Stay fresh. Stay confident. Stay Faded.

-NIKKOLE ABILA

Certified Hair System Specialists · Arvada, Colorado

Hair Loss Solutions for Men

At our Arvada, Colorado barbershop, certified hair system specialist Nikkole helps men dealing with thinning hair, receding hairlines, and hair loss find a natural-looking, non-surgical solution.

Do you offer non-surgical hair systems in Arvada?

Yes. Stay Faded offers same-day, non-surgical hair systems for men dealing with thinning, receding hairlines, and hair loss. Start with a consultation so Nikkole can recommend the right fit and style.

Do you offer hair system installation?

Yes. We can help with the consultation, system selection, installation, cut, blend, and styling so you leave with a natural-looking result that fits your lifestyle.

Are you hair loss specialists?

We are certified hair system specialists who help men dealing with thinning hair, receding hairlines, and hair loss explore a confident, non-surgical solution.

Can I shower, swim, and work out with a hair system?

Yes. A properly fitted hair system is designed for normal life, including showering, swimming, working out, sweating, and styling.

How long does a hair system last?

Hair-system longevity depends on lifestyle, skin, system type, and maintenance, but on average a hair system lasts six to seven months. Stay Faded will explain the expected upkeep during your consultation.
